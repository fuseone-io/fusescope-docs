**Use this guide when** you want one page that shows the health of your services at a
glance, to keep open on a screen or to share with your team.

You will build the dashboard below from trace data: how much each service does, how
often it fails, and how slow it is. No metrics setup is needed.

![The finished HotROD overview dashboard: a Spans per service line chart, an Errors per service bar chart and a P99 latency by service table.](../../../assets/dashboards/dashboard-detail.png)

You need the Editor or Admin [role](/administration/users-and-roles/#roles).

## Create the dashboard

<Steps>

1. Open **Dashboards** in the sidebar and click **New dashboard**.
2. On the **Blank** tab, replace `Sample Dashboard` in **Title** with a name, such as
   `HotROD overview`, and add an optional **Description**. In **Tags**, type
   `team:demo` and press Enter.
3. Click **Create dashboard**. The new dashboard opens on **Welcome to your new
   dashboard**.

</Steps>

![An empty dashboard: Welcome to your new dashboard, with a Configure your new dashboard step and an Add panels step with New Panel.](../../../assets/dashboards/dashboard-empty.png)

## Panel 1: spans per service

A [panel](/reference/glossary/#panel) is one chart on the dashboard.

<Steps>

1. Click **New Panel**. In the **New panel** picker, click **Time Series**, then
   **Add panel**. The [panel editor](/dashboards/panel-editor/) opens:
   the query on the left, a preview of the chart in the middle and the panel settings
   on the right.
2. In the query card, change **Source** from **Metrics** to **Traces**. The
   aggregation is `count()`: the number of [spans](/reference/glossary/#span).
3. Open **Result**, click **Group By** and pick `service.name`, to draw one line per
   service.
4. In **Legend format**, enter `{{service.name}}`, so each line is labelled with its
   service name.
5. On the right, set **Title** to `Spans per service`.
6. Click **Run Query** to check the chart, then **Save changes**.

</Steps>

![The panel editor: a Traces count() query grouped by service.name with a legend format that names each line after its service on the left, the Spans per service chart in the middle, and Panel Details on the right.](../../../assets/dashboards/panel-editor-new-panel.png)

## Panel 2: errors per service

<Steps>

1. Click **New Panel** at the top of the dashboard, pick **Bar Chart** and click
   **Add panel**.
2. Change **Source** to **Traces** and type this filter in **Where**:

   ```text
   has_error = true
   ```

3. In **Result**, group by `service.name` and set the legend to `{{service.name}}`.
   Set the title to `Errors per service`.
4. Click **Run Query**, then **Save changes**.

</Steps>

## Panel 3: P99 latency per service

<Steps>

1. Click **New Panel**, pick **Table** and click **Add panel**.
2. Change **Source** to **Traces**. Replace `count()` with
   `p99(duration_nano)`, the duration that 99 in 100 spans stay under.
3. In **Result**, group by `service.name`. Set the title to
   `P99 latency by service`.
4. Durations are stored in nanoseconds. On the right, open **Formatting & Units**,
   and under **Column units** pick **Nanoseconds** for the `p99(duration_nano)` column. The
   table now shows values such as `737.86 ms`.
5. Click **Run Query**, then **Save changes**.

</Steps>

## Arrange and share

- Drag a panel by its header to move it, and drag its bottom-right corner to resize it.
  The layout is saved when you let go.
- Change the [time range](/reference/glossary/#time-range) at the top of
  the page; every panel follows it.
- Click **Share** (the globe icon at the top right), then **Copy page link**. Anyone
  with access to FuseScope who opens it sees the same dashboard and time range.
- To group panels later, use **Actions** › **New section**. See
  [Sections](/dashboards/dashboards/#sections).

## Expected result

A dashboard with three panels that update with the time range. Open it from
**Dashboards** at any time; click the pin icon on its row to find it in the **Pinned**
view.

<Aside type="tip">
  To show one service at a time, add a variable: click **Add variable** under the
  title, create a **Dynamic** variable on `service.name`, and use **Apply to panels**.
  See [Dashboard variables](/dashboards/variables/) and the
  [worked example](/dashboards/dashboards/#example-filter-a-dashboard-by-service).
</Aside>
