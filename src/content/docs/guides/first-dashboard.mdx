**Use this guide when** you want one page that shows the health of your services at a
glance, to keep open on a screen or to share with your team.

You will build the dashboard below from trace data: how much each service does, how
often it fails, and how slow it is. No metrics setup is needed.

![The finished HotROD overview dashboard: a Spans per service line chart, an Errors per service bar chart and a P99 latency by service table.](../../../assets/dashboards/dashboard-detail.png)

You need the Editor or Admin [role](/fusescope-docs/administration/users-and-roles/#roles).

## Create the dashboard

<Steps>

1. Open **Dashboards** in the sidebar and click **New dashboard**.
2. On the **Blank** tab, enter a **Title**, such as `HotROD overview`, and an
   optional **Description**. Add a tag such as `team:demo` and press Enter.
3. Click **Create dashboard**. The empty dashboard opens.

</Steps>

## Panel 1: spans per service

A [panel](/fusescope-docs/reference/glossary/#panel) is one chart on the dashboard.

<Steps>

1. Click **New Panel**, pick **Time Series** and click **Add panel**. The panel editor
   opens: the chart preview on top, the query below and the panel options on the right.
2. In the query, change the data source from **Metrics** to **Traces**. The
   aggregation is `count()`: the number of [spans](/fusescope-docs/reference/glossary/#span).
3. Click **Group By** and pick `service.name`, to draw one line per service.
4. Click **Legend format** and enter `{{service.name}}`, so each line is labelled with
   its service name.
5. On the right, set **Title** to `Spans per service`.
6. Click **Run Query** to check the chart, then **Save changes**.

</Steps>

![Panel editor: a Traces query with count() grouped by service.name and a legend format that names each line after its service, the Spans per service chart above it, and Panel Details on the right.](../../../assets/dashboards/panel-editor.png)

## Panel 2: errors per service

<Steps>

1. Click **New Panel**, pick **Bar Chart** and click **Add panel**.
2. Change the data source to **Traces** and type this filter in the search box:

   ```text
   has_error = true
   ```

3. Group by `service.name`, set the legend to `{{service.name}}` and the title to
   `Errors per service`.
4. Click **Run Query**, then **Save changes**.

</Steps>

## Panel 3: P99 latency per service

<Steps>

1. Click **New Panel**, pick **Table** and click **Add panel**.
2. Change the data source to **Traces**. Replace `count()` with
   `p99(duration_nano)`, the duration that 99 in 100 spans stay under.
3. Group by `service.name` and set the title to `P99 latency by service`.
4. Durations are stored in nanoseconds. Open **Formatting & Units**, and under
   **Column units** pick **Nanoseconds** for the `p99(duration_nano)` column. The
   table now shows values such as `737.86 ms`.
5. Click **Run Query**, then **Save changes**.

</Steps>

## Arrange and share

- Drag a panel by its title to move it, and drag its bottom-right corner to resize it.
  The layout is saved when you let go.
- Change the [time range](/fusescope-docs/reference/glossary/#time-range) at the top of
  the dashboard; every panel follows it.
- Click the share button (the globe icon) to copy a link to the dashboard. Anyone with
  access to FuseScope who opens it sees the same dashboard and time range.

## Expected result

A dashboard with three panels that update with the time range. Open it from
**Dashboards** at any time; pin it with the pin icon on its row to keep it at the top.

<Aside type="tip">
  To show one service at a time, add a variable: click **Add variable**, create a
  **Dynamic** variable on `service.name`, and use **Apply to panels**. See
  [Dashboard variables](/fusescope-docs/dashboards/variables/).
</Aside>
